## Structural patterns (rewrite)

- Opening with a broad statement about the industry before naming the subject.
- Triads of adjectives for rhythm ("fast, safe, and simple").
- Sentence-final dramatic kickers ("And that changes everything.").
- Rhetorical questions as transitions.
- Restating the thesis in grander terms at the end.
- Lists whose items begin with a bolded phrase followed by a colon.
- Contrast framing "It's not X. It's Y." across two sentences.
- Excessive em dashes used as a universal connector; prefer commas, colons or a new sentence. One em dash per paragraph at most.
- "Whether ... or ..." enumerations that add no cases.
- Headings that are questions.
- "Note:" or "Important:" call-outs; state the rule in the main text.

## Punctuation

- No emoji. No exclamation marks. No arrows in prose. Curly quotes are not required; straight quotes are acceptable. Ellipses only inside quotations.
